# 📊 Bank Customer Segmentation Analysis

## 🎯 Project Overview
Customer behaviour varies significantly across a banking customer base. Treating all customers as a single population can result in inefficient marketing, weak retention strategies, and poor allocation of customer engagement resources.

This project applies RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis to a dataset containing 1M+ customer transaction records to identify behavioural and value-based customer segments.

The analysis transforms transaction-level data into customer-level behavioural metrics, assigns RFM scores, classifies customers into actionable segments, and visualises segment performance through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The objective is to provide a structured analytical framework for understanding customer value, engagement, and purchasing behaviour and to support more targeted customer retention and commercial strategies.

## ⚠️ Business Problem
Banks handle large volumes of customer transaction data but often struggle to translate it into actionable insights. 
They have limited visibility into:
Which customers generate the greatest value
Which customers demonstrate strong engagement
Which customers may require re-engagement
How customer value is distributed across behavioural segments
How customer segments contribute to overall revenue
Where targeted retention and customer engagement strategies could be applied

Without customer-level segmentation, customer engagement initiatives may be applied too broadly.
The analysis therefore focuses on converting transactional behaviour into actionable customer segments.

## Analytical Framework

The core analytical framework is RFM analysis.
RFM measures customer behaviour using three dimensions:
| Metric    | Definition                                  | Business Meaning                    |
|-----------|---------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Recency   | Time since the customer's most recent transaction | Customer engagement / activity      |
| Frequency | Number of transactions made by the customer | Engagement and purchasing consistency |
| Monetary  | Total monetary value generated by the customer | Customer value                      |

**Recency**
Measures how recently a customer interacted with the business.
A more recent transaction generally indicates stronger current engagement.
Recency = Reference Date - Last Transaction Date

**Frequency**
Measures the number of transactions associated with each customer during the observation period.
Higher frequency indicates more consistent transaction activity.
Frequency = COUNT(Transaction)

**Monetary**
Measures the total transaction value associated with each customer.
Higher monetary value indicates greater historical financial contri
Monetary = SUM(Transaction Value)

**Combined Interpretation**
RFM should not be interpreted as three isolated metrics.
The combination provides a more complete behavioural profile.

For example:
High Recency
+
High Frequency
+
High Monetary
=
Highly valuable and recently engaged customer

Whereas:

Low Recency
+
Low Frequency
+
Historically High Monetary
=
Potential high-value reactivation opportunity

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- A small group of customers contributes to a large portion of total transaction value  
- Revenue is high, but individual transaction values are relatively low  
- Customers are concentrated in specific geographic regions 
-Transaction activity peaks at certain times, suggesting opportunities for time-based promotions  
- Some customers show low spending but high balances, indicating untapped potential

## 💡Recommendations:
- Targeted marketing: personalize campaigns for high- value and high-potential customers to maximize ROI
- Engage low-frequency cusotmers with incentives and loyalty programs
- Focus marketing and resources on high-performing regions while improving underperforming areas
- Schedule promotions and notifications during peak transaction periods
- Offer tailored financial products based on customer segment behavior
